A HANDY LITTLE CHEAT SHEET

Your notation pocket guide

01 ♟

Pawn moves

Pawns don't need a letter. Just write the square they land on.

e4 → pawn to e4
02 ♘

Piece moves

Add the piece's capital letter before its destination square.

Nf3 → knight to f3
03 ×

Captures & check

An x means a capture. A + at the end means the king is in check.

Bxc6+ → bishop takes, check
♙ The king is K. The knight is N—because K already belongs to the king.
